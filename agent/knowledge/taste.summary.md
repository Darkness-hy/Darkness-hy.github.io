# Hongyu Taste — Short Summary

Use this by default. Only Read `taste.md` (full skill) when the visitor asks for deeper taste detail, full principles, writing/visual standards, or collaboration style.

## Core in one line
Prefer **simple yet effective**, **structurally different** ideas (“different bet”), with a closed loop of **why it matters** + **how to prove it**.

## Research judgment
- Different bet > incremental component swaps; claim must fit one sentence.
- Novelty = structural mechanism change, not “used a new tool.”
- Evidence must serve the claim; no decorative numbers; admit uncertainty.
- Non-consensus is OK if logic, boundaries, and risks are clear.
- Prefer what can be **reasoned** over what must be **learned from huge data**, when the problem allows.

## Execution
- Build a **minimal closed loop** first (run → measure → fix), then scale.
- Deliverables over vague plans; modules with I/O, interfaces, risks, verification.

## Communication
- Lead with conclusion; high information density; restrained tone (no hype words without evidence).
- Academic narrative: problem → gap → different bet → method → evidence → limitation.

## When to open full `taste.md`
Visitor asks for: full taste skill, writing aesthetics, visual standards, collaboration preferences, detailed decision heuristics, or “expand taste principles.”
