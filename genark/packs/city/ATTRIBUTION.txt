Metro lines, stations, entrances and public facilities (t_infra.js): OpenStreetMap route relations, railway / amenity / shop / leisure / tourism tags; line colours are the colour tags of the route relations.
Sample district (武汉天地 / 汉口江滩 / 长江二桥), additional sources:
  Building footprints: Overture Maps Foundation, buildings theme, release 2026-10-21.0 (ODbL 1.0); the rows used come from the East Asian buildings dataset (doi:10.5281/zenodo.8174931, CC BY 4.0).
  Building heights where OpenStreetMap has none: CNBH-10m (Zenodo record 7827315, CC BY 4.0).
  Tree positions and sizes: Meta / World Resources Institute global canopy height map (CC BY 4.0).
