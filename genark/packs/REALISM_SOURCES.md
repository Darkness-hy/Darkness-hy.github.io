# People and film-audio attribution

The `people_scan.glb` asset comes from the publicly served CROSSTOWN front-end mirror supplied in `project-ref`. The original pack is retained as a reference, not loaded by the current crowd.

`asian_ped_0.glb` through `asian_ped_3.glb` are new character variants generated by `tools/make_asian_people.py` from the MakeHuman / MPFB2 base mesh, East Asian macro targets, game-engine rig and weights (CC0). Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/master/src/mpfb/data . Clothing, hair, eyes, four appearance variants and the walking / idle / talking / sitting / cycling / driving animation clips were generated locally. These are parametric character models, not scans of identifiable people.

Film foley in `film/audio/sources`:
- `keyboard.ogg`: Yuyudevil, "Keyboard noise", public domain, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Keyboard_noise.ogg . Cropped and level-adjusted.
- `rain.ogg`: Effib, "Sound of rain", CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sound_of_rain.ogg . High-pass filtered, layered and level-adjusted; this adapted audio is distributed under the same licence.
- `mouse.ogg`: Mathieu Kappler, "Computer mouse clics", CC BY-SA 4.0, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_mouse_clics.ogg . Cropped and level-adjusted; this adapted audio is distributed under the same licence.


- Original human scans: HSRD-100, Digital Reality Lab (HumanScanRepository), https://huggingface.co/datasets/digitalrealitylab/HSRD-100
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- CROSSTOWN source: https://nyc.somethingbig.ai/
- Original asset changes: simplified, cleaned, de-lit, rigged and animated. The people shown are scan models and do not endorse this project.
- Changes for LAVIRA: repacked as a local script asset, scaled to the simulator's people, animation tracks filtered per character, eye and hair material handling adjusted. Simulation behaviour and camera logic are separate.

The original animation database attribution also states: motion data obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu, supported by NSF EIA-0196217; BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com).

Facade panel sources and their CC BY / CC BY-SA terms are listed separately in `PANEL_SOURCES.md`. The `facade_atlas_d.jpg` atlas is an adapted, downsampled arrangement of those panels and retains the corresponding per-panel licences, including CC BY-SA 4.0 where applicable.

The daytime sky is Poly Haven's `qwantani_noon_puresky`, CC0, distributed in the CROSSTOWN public asset set. It is blended with the simulator's own sunrise, sunset, weather and night sky rather than used as a fixed background.

The scanned leaf texture comes from the CROSSTOWN street-tree assets. The original asset attribution lists tree, bark and prop textures and models from Poly Haven and ambientCG, CC0.
